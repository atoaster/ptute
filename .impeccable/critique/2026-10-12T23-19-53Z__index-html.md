## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** structurally authored, superficially generic. The composition belongs to this product only: a fixed G3–C5 keyboard with a dot on middle C, a 4rem letter with a 1.5rem octave digit, note letters as the progress bar, a one-slot status strip that never says "wrong", a tuner that latches the last A. The skin is the quiet-utility default (system sans, selection blue, 6px radius, hairline list); DESIGN.md chooses it deliberately, but the lit key in UI-selection blue reads as a highlighted control rather than a key under light, and the wordmark and favicon carry none of the product's one visual idea.

**Deterministic scan:** 0 findings, 0 advisories across 13 scans (static index.html + src, a `--no-config` control, and all three routes rendered at 1280×800 and 390×844). Console clean on every route; no horizontal overflow at 390px. The detector's sensitivity was confirmed on a deliberately bad fixture (3 findings), so the clean result is genuine. Caveat: the rendered scans saw Practice and Tune idle, not listening.

**Visual overlays:** not available (headless environment, no user-visible tab); fallback signal is the clean CLI + URL scans above.

## Overall Impression
The core loop is right and the discipline is real. The weaknesses are all one family: the app is quiet exactly where a nervous beginner needs to be sure they were heard, and the hero can sit below the fold on the device that actually sits on the music stand.

## What's Working
- Core-loop timing is designed, not accidental: onset-only judging, the 150 ms blink that makes a repeated note a new ask, the ≥600 ms hold on ✓, the "Play again" caption on repeats.
- One status slot, one voice, no red: idle, listening, hearing, correct, wrong, five mic errors and completion all live in one place; errors name the problem and the fix; the keyboard dims in every non-listening state.
- Native-control accessibility: tab order equals reading order, focus lands on the h1 per route and on the next live button after Start, the whole flow is keyboard-completable, colour is never the sole carrier.

## Priority Issues
1. **[P1] Wrong-note feedback is the smallest text on the page and evaporates.** Measured lifetime of "Heard C♯4, not C4." was 1.16 s at 14px grey under a 160px keyboard; the player who looked at their hands looks up to "Listening…" and the same lit key. Fix: hold the sentence and the grey key until the next strike (2 s floor) and render it in `--ink`. `/impeccable clarify`
2. **[P1] The hero is below the fold on landscape phones and at 200% zoom.** ~286px of header, title, hint, strip and big note sit above the keyboard; at 844×390 the keyboard is cut and Start listening is off-screen; at 200% zoom (512×400) Start is at y=470. Fix: keyboard directly under the title, big note and strip beneath it, and cap the keyboard height with a vh term. `/impeccable layout`
3. **[P2] Two beginner dead ends have no in-app help.** A wrong octave repeats "Heard C3, not C4." forever (off-keyboard notes mark nothing), and a quiet or distant mic sits on "Listening…" indefinitely. Fix: mirror the semitone rule with "Same note, a different octave. Middle C is the key with the dot." after 2 octave misses; teach the digit once in lesson 1's hint. `/impeccable clarify`
4. **[P2] The idle screen issues two instructions at once.** "Play" + 64px "C4" + lit key at full strength above "Start listening". Fix: dim the big note with the keyboard in idle and error states. `/impeccable onboard`
5. **[P2] "Tune" and "Play again" are overloaded.** "Tune" beside "Twinkle Twinkle" reads as a song; "Play again" is both the repeat caption and the restart button. Fix: "Tuner" everywhere; completion button "Start over". `/impeccable clarify`

## Persona Red Flags
**Jordan (first-timer):** hint says "middle C", screen says "C4", nothing links them; plays before pressing Start; first miss vanishes while looking at hands; wrong octave on lesson 1 is a dead end; denied-mic copy points at an address-bar icon iPhones don't have.
**Casey (phone on the music stand, interrupted):** landscape puts the keyboard at y=286 and Start at y≈530; 14px status is unreadable from the bench; header links are ~24px tall tap targets out of the thumb zone; an app switch stops the mic (one tap to recover, progress kept).
**Sam (screen reader / keyboard / low vision):** flow is keyboard-completable with correct focus moves; but each accepted note fires 3–4 announcements from two polite regions within 150 ms; the reference input announces without its unit; 200% zoom puts Start below the fold.

## Minor Observations
- Direct URL load shows the h1 focus ring (reads as a text field); mouse navigation already suppresses it.
- `.btn` has no hover/active state.
- Tune: starting the mic pushes the Reference block down ~110px.
- Practice → Tune → "← Lessons" loses the lesson; a remembered last lesson is a ten-line fix §10 does not forbid.
- Favicon is a plain accent circle; the product's mark is a black dot on a white key.
- Lesson 8 "Happy Birthday" is one phrase (6 notes); the title promises the song.
- The strip could carry phrase gaps (chunking failed the cognitive-load check: up to 15 ungrouped letters).
